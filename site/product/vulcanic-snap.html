About this work

These art painting have been hand created a generative adversarial network algorithm trained on Vulcano photos. By applying abstraction, Vulcanic Snap seduces the viewer into a world of ongoing equilibrium and the interval that articulates the stream of daily events.

This ai art is signed by Art Supreme. It is made on canvas and it comes with the original Certificate of Authenticity.
