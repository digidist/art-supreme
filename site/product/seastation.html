About this work

Seastation is presenting the dynamics of landscape, including the manipulation of its effects and the limits of spectacle based on our assumptions of what landscape means to us. Rather than presenting a factual reality, an illusion is fabricated to conjure the realms of our imagination.

This ai art is signed by Art Supreme. It is made on canvas and it comes with the original Certificate of Authenticity.
